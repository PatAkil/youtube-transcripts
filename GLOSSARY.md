# YouTube Transcripts

A corpus of English transcripts from other creators' YouTube videos and podcasts, collected so the sponsor segments in them can be studied: to see which sponsors partner with which channels, and to see how other creators write and place their sponsor reads.

## Language

**Video**:
A single YouTube video identified by its YouTube video ID. Podcast episodes published on YouTube are Videos too.
_Avoid_: Episode, clip, upload

**Channel**:
The YouTube channel that published a Video. Always someone else's channel, never our own.
_Avoid_: Creator, account

**Followed Channel**:
A Channel on the committed list that a sync walks to pull its newest Videos into the Corpus.
_Avoid_: Subscription, tracked channel, source

**Transcript**:
The timestamped spoken words of a Video as provided by YouTube's caption track, in English. The Transcript is always sourced from YouTube, never from our own speech-to-text.
_Avoid_: Captions, subtitles, subs

**Sponsor**:
A brand that pays a Channel to be promoted in a Video.
_Avoid_: Brand, advertiser, partner

**Sponsor Slot**:
A contiguous window of time in a Video given over to sponsor reads. A Slot holds one or more Sponsor Segments, one per Sponsor read back to back. Typically placed just after the intro, or mid-episode in podcasts.
_Avoid_: Sponsor block, ad break

**Sponsor Segment**:
One Sponsor's paid read within a Sponsor Slot. Self-promotion (the creator's own course, merch, Patreon) and engagement reminders are not Sponsor Segments. Two reads for the same Sponsor in one Video are two Segments.
_Avoid_: Ad, ad read, promo, sponsorship

**Sponsorship**:
The fact that a Sponsor has partnered with a Channel, evidenced by one or more Sponsor Segments in that Channel's Videos.
_Avoid_: Deal, partnership

**Corpus**:
The whole collection of Transcripts and their Sponsor Segments gathered by this project.
_Avoid_: Library, database, archive
